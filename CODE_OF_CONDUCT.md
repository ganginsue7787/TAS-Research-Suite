# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the **TAS Research Suite**, pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for researchers, practitioners, and students across all disciplines.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behaviour

- **Be respectful** — Treat all participants with dignity and professionalism
- **Be constructive** — Offer evidence-based feedback, not personal criticism
- **Focus on scientific discussion** — Disagreements should be about methods and results, not individuals
- **Respect different viewpoints** — Different application domains and theoretical perspectives are welcome
- **Support reproducibility and transparency** — Share code, data, and methodology honestly
- **Give credit** — Cite prior work appropriately; acknowledge contributions from others
- **Be patient** — Not everyone has the same background or availability

### Unacceptable Behaviour

- **Harassment** of any kind, in any communication channel
- **Personal attacks** on contributors or researchers
- **Discrimination** based on any personal characteristic
- **Spam** — irrelevant promotional content, unsolicited advertising
- **Misrepresentation of research results** — fabrication, falsification, or selective reporting of data
- **Plagiarism** — presenting others' work as your own without attribution
- **Threatening behaviour** — implied or explicit threats of harm

---

## Enforcement

### Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Actions

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviours that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a GitHub Issue labelled `[conduct]` or by contacting the project maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Code reviews
- Project documentation
- Any official project communications (email, conference presentations citing this repository)
